# Koin App — Copa do Mundo 2026: Destrava o Jogo
## Strategic Recommendation Document
**Campaign period:** June 8 – July 20, 2026 (43 days)
**Promotion budget:** R$113,000
**Date:** May 26, 2026

---

## Framing note

The concept "Destrava o Jogo" is the right call. The Copa is a cultural mass moment for exactly the classes C/D/E audience Koin serves. But it is a risky campaign anchor for a BNPL product because the primary conversion bottleneck is not awareness or desire. It is structural: roughly 65% of registered users never receive a credit limit and cannot buy. No promotion mechanic fixes that.

The R$113K must be concentrated where it can actually move the needle: converting the segment that already has a limit but has not yet purchased. Every mechanic that does not touch that segment first is a vanity spend.

---

## 1. User Segmentation for Promotions

| Segment | Description | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| A — Limit, 0 purchases | Approved limit, never activated | CRITICAL |
| B — Activated, 1-2 purchases | Light users, frequency gap | HIGH |
| C — Power users, 5+ purchases | Loyal buyers | MEDIUM |
| D — No limit approved | Cannot buy at all | LOW — engagement only |

**Why Segment A is the only real conversion opportunity:** They have already cleared the hardest gate (credit evaluation). The activation gap is behavioral, not structural. Copa urgency and flash pricing are the correct levers here.

**Critical: Segment D users cannot buy.** Any promotion mechanic that reaches them and implies they can buy generates frustration and churn. Segment D = engagement-only, never confused with activation spend.

### Mechanic-to-segment mapping

| Mechanic | Primary segment | Secondary | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apita o Pix (90-min flash) | A | B | Urgency and scarcity work on first-time buyers. Short window forces decision. |
| Se o Brasil ganhar (26% OFF next day) | A | B | Post-victory emotional high is the highest-intent moment for a first purchase. |
| A cada gol do Brasil | B | C | Engagement/loyalty tool, not an acquisition trigger. |
| Craques da Semana | C | B | Frequency leaderboard for already-activated users. Referral flywheel. |
| Bolão Koin | D | A (secondary) | Engagement for users who cannot buy yet. Keeps Segment D warm. |

## 4. Mechanic Refinement

### Apita o Pix — **KEEP. Primary engine.**

Copa-wide (runs on every match, not just Brazil) is its key structural advantage. Works regardless of Brazil's results.

**Modification:** Extend window to 120 minutes, not 90. Many purchases happen during halftime or immediately after the final whistle, not mid-play.

**Second-order effect:** When Segment A converts on Game 1, they become Segment B. By Week 3-4, shift primary targeting from Segment A to Segment B for recurrence. Plan this audience switch explicitly or you'll spend the whole campaign re-targeting already-converted users with acquisition messaging.

---

### Se o Brasil ganhar — **KEEP with guardrails.**

The morning after a Brazil victory is the highest-emotion, highest-intent consumer moment in Brazilian culture for this audience. "26%" references Brazil's Copa history — specificity signals care.

**Modification:** Clarify the benefit trigger internally before launch. Does "26% OFF" apply to Koin's fee, partner merchant prices, or cashback? Koin-side fee reduction or cashback is operationally cleanest. Ambiguity destroys trust and generates WhatsApp complaints.

**If Brazil is eliminated:** Write the "Brasil fora, mas o jogo do Koin continua" messaging before the tournament starts. Do not wait until elimination.

---

### A cada gol do Brasil — **MODIFY or absorb into Apita o Pix.**

Tiered reward structures are too complex to communicate via push notification during an active match. If the team wants this mechanic, simplify to one rule: "Brasil marcou? Você tem 26 minutos para comprar com desconto especial." This collapses into Apita o Pix, removes tiered complexity, creates a single memorable trigger. The 26-minute window is thematic.

---

### Craques da Semana — **KEEP with structural changes.**

**Drop the 60% cashback option.** It rewards ticket size inflation, not frequency. Replace with tiered vouchers: R$100 / R$180 / R$260 for top 3 frequency tiers weekly. Cap at 10-15 winners per week, announced every Monday via WhatsApp. Regional announcements ("alguém do meu estado ganhou") generate social proof that travels through WhatsApp communities.

**Anti-fraud rules (mandatory before launch):**
- Minimum R$80 purchase value per qualifying transaction
- Minimum 48-hour interval between qualifying purchases
- Maximum 3 qualifying transactions per CPF per week
- All winners subject to manual review before voucher distribution

---

### Bolão Koin — **KEEP as low-cost Segment D layer. Legal review required.**

**Modify the prize:** Not cashback (Segment D cannot buy). Change to: R$20 voucher activated on first purchase, or a credit review priority notification ("Você pode ter sido pré-aprovado — verifique agora"). Converts engagement into an activation trigger.

**Legal risk — CRITICAL:** Bolão Koin must not resemble sports betting under Brazilian law (Lei 14.790/2023). Must be structured as a free-to-enter promotional contest (promoção comercial) under SUSEP. **Legal sign-off required before June 8.** If legal cannot clear this before June 1, drop it.

## 5. The One Bet — Highest-Leverage Paid Media Placement

**Click-to-WhatsApp ads via Meta Ads Manager, targeting Segment A lookalike audiences, concentrated in the 72-hour window surrounding each Brazil game.**

**The rationale:**
- Classes C/D/E in Brazil treat WhatsApp as primary digital infrastructure. An ad that opens directly into WhatsApp removes every friction layer.
- Segment A is a first-party retargetable audience — Koin has their data. A lookalike built from activated users will be far more precise and cheaper per qualified click than interest-based targeting.
- Click-to-WhatsApp is under-utilized in Brazilian fintech vs. app install campaigns — lower CPMs for the same audience quality.
- The Copa creates a precise emotional targeting window. Copa interest + geographic concentration (SP, RJ, BA, MG, DF, PR, RS) + game-window timing = Koin's exact audience at peak intent.
- The WhatsApp entry point closes the loop in the same session: "Quer usar o Koin no próximo jogo? Aqui está o seu desconto de 26%."

**What to avoid:** Do not run brand-awareness video ads with a limited budget. Segment A already knows Koin — they installed the app and were approved. Awareness is not their bottleneck. Direct response is.

## 6. Risks and Guardrails

### Risk 1 — Brazil exits before the Quarterfinals

**Probability: Moderate.** Brazil has not won the Copa since 2002. Round of 16 elimination is a realistic scenario.

**Impact:** Se o Brasil ganhar loses all remaining windows. ~R$40K in planned spend and the campaign's emotional spine collapses.

**Mitigation:**
- Hold 40% of Se o Brasil ganhar budget until Brazil clears Round of 16
- Write Brazil elimination messaging before the tournament — "O jogo do Koin não para" must deploy within 2 hours of elimination
- Reinforce internally that Apita o Pix is Copa-wide. Reallocate eliminated mechanics' budgets to extending Apita o Pix into QF/SF of other nations
- **Second-order opportunity:** If Brazil exits and Koin keeps running, "Koin está com você ganhando ou perdendo" becomes a loyalty signal this demographic shares

---

### Risk 2 — Match-day notification failure

**Probability: Moderate-high.** Brazil games generate the highest simultaneous mobile usage peaks in Brazil. Push notification open rates drop when everyone's phone is buzzing.

**Impact:** Failed Apita o Pix notification is a trust-destruction event. Users primed by pre-game communications who receive nothing churn at higher rates than users who never received the campaign.

**Mitigation:**
- Run end-to-end test of automation during a non-Brazil Copa match before Game 1 (live dress rehearsal with real sends)
- Set notification send-time 5 minutes before kickoff, not at kickoff
- Build manual fallback: one team member with direct send access if automation fails
- Submit WhatsApp HSM templates for Meta approval at least 5 business days before June 11. Pre-approved templates partially bypass Copa-period throttling.

---

### Risk 3 — Fraud abuse on Craques da Semana and regulatory challenge on Bolão

**Probability: Moderate.** High-value iFood vouchers attract coordinated CPF abuse. Bolão carries regulatory misclassification risk.

**Impact:** Fraudulent winners inflate costs with zero GMV return and destroy social proof mid-campaign. A regulatory challenge during the Copa damages brand trust with a credit-sensitive audience at the worst possible moment.

**Mitigation:**
- Craques anti-fraud rules must be published in a formal Regulamento de Promoção before any public communication (required under Decreto 70.951/72 and SUSEP normatives)
- Bolão Koin: legal must issue formal clearance before June 1 or the mechanic is dropped
- Budget 5-7 business days for legal review — which means review must begin **no later than May 28**
- A fraud incident mid-campaign doesn't just cost money — it poisons the WhatsApp social proof layer that is the most valuable earned channel Koin has

---

## Strategic Summary

If Brazil wins the Copa, this campaign will look like a stroke of genius. If Brazil exits in the Quarterfinals, Koin should still hit its Activation targets — because the strategy was never actually about Brazil winning.

The discipline required: treat Apita o Pix as the primary engine (Copa-wide, always on), Se o Brasil ganhar as the emotional accelerant (contingency-budgeted), Craques da Semana as the retention flywheel (fraud-protected), Bolão Koin as the low-cost Segment D warmup layer (legally cleared), and A cada gol as an optional organic layer with minimal budget.

That is the test of a good Copa campaign for a financial product: **it works either way.**
