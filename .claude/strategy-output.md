# Koin App — Focused Marketing Strategy
**Date:** May 2026
**Scope:** 90-day actionable strategy grounded in Koin's funnel, channels, and KPIs

---

## Strategic Frame: Where the Real Problem Lives

Before recommending tactics, a constraint diagnosis matters. Koin's funnel has a hard structural gate: only ~35% of users who complete Cadastro receive a credit limit. That means roughly 65% of acquired users cannot activate — full stop. No marketing campaign can convert someone with no limit.

This has two implications:

**1. Acquisition quality matters more than acquisition volume.** Spending more on Meta/Google to bring in users who fail credit eval is pure CAC waste. The top strategic priority is shifting the channel mix toward higher-quality install sources (B2B referrals, organic) that have demonstrably better credit eval pass rates — even at lower volume.

**2. The no-limit segment is an untapped retention asset.** Users who registered but got no limit are warm, showed intent, and may become eligible later. Without a nurturing strategy, Koin loses them permanently. This is a medium-term unlock.

Every recommendation below is filtered through this frame.

## Part 1: Top 5 Marketing Ideas from the 139-Idea Library

### Idea 1 — Viral Loops / Referral Program (#93)
**Adapted for Koin:** A structured "Indica e Ganha" mechanic where an activated user refers a friend. The benefit — for both referrer and new user — is only unlocked when the referred user completes their first purchase, not just installs or registers.

**Why it fits:** Classes C/D/E audiences in Brazil operate on deep trust networks — family WhatsApp groups, neighborhood Facebook communities, peer word-of-mouth. A referral from a trusted person carries conversion power no paid ad can match. Critically, referred users are pre-qualified: they come in warm with a concrete use case in mind and likely share similar credit profiles to the referrer, improving eval pass rates.

**How to start:**
1. Define the reward: limit increase for the referrer is preferred (costs less than cashback, drives engagement, is product-native)
2. Build referral link generation inside the app, surfaced immediately post first purchase (peak satisfaction moment)
3. Pre-write WhatsApp-native sharing copy — one-tap forwarding, do not rely on users composing their own message

**Expected outcome:** 15–25% of activated users attempt a referral. Referred installs convert to activation at 1.5–2x the rate of paid channels.

**Resources needed:** Engineering (2–3 sprints), CRM (WhatsApp trigger post-activation), Product/Marketing alignment on reward design.

### Idea 2 — Onboarding / Lifecycle Sequences (#48)
**Adapted for Koin:** Two distinct CRM tracks via Email + Push + WhatsApp. Track A: users with a limit who have not yet activated (5–7 messages over 14 days). Track B: users with no limit who may become eligible after 30–60 days (reactivation at Day 30 and Day 60 post-rejection).

**Why it fits:** There is currently no active nurturing strategy for the no-limit segment — a significant leak. A user who registered and was rejected is not dead; bureau data and income context can shift within weeks. For users who have a limit but have not purchased, the gap between approval and first purchase is where Koin loses users to friction and distraction.

**How to start:**
1. Map the current state: what messages (if any) are sent between limit approval and Day 14? Identify the gap
2. Deploy a 5-message activation sprint: Day 0 (limit approved + how to use it), Day 2 (step-by-step first purchase on Shopee), Day 5 (use on iFood today), Day 9 (urgency frame — limit unused), Day 14 (social proof from a real user)
3. Build the no-limit reactivation: Day 30 ("We review limits periodically — check if yours changed") and Day 60 (same, with a concrete use case)

**Expected outcome:** 8–15 percentage point lift in activation rate for the with-limit cohort. 5–10% reactivation of no-limit cohort within 60 days.

**Resources needed:** CRM/lifecycle team (2 weeks to build), copywriter (Portuguese, class C/D/E tone — direct, warm, concrete), WhatsApp Business API access.

### Idea 3 — ASO + Competitor Comparison Content (#1, #11)
**Adapted for Koin:** Two connected plays. First, App Store Optimization targeting the searches Koin's audience types when looking for BNPL alternatives ("parcelamento sem cartao de credito", "Pix parcelado", "credito para quem nao tem cartao"). Second, one SEO landing page targeting comparison queries like "alternativa ao Nubank Parcelado" or "como parcelar sem cartao via Pix".

**Why it fits:** The app store search bar is where classes C/D/E users research financial apps — not Google. Optimizing the listing title, subtitle, keyword field, and screenshots for the right terms is a zero-cost activity that compounds permanently. A large portion of Koin's addressable audience searches for Nubank Parcelado or Neon Parcelado first; being present when users discover the category is the play, not outspending dominant brands.

**How to start:**
1. Run keyword research in the App Store (AppFollow or manual competitor gap analysis). Identify 10–15 high-volume, low-competition terms in Portuguese
2. Rewrite App Store and Google Play listing: title contains primary keyword, subtitle hits secondary terms, first 3 description lines carry the value prop before the fold
3. Create one landing page targeting "como parcelar sem cartão de crédito no Brasil" — 800 words, direct language, CTA to download

**Expected outcome:** 20–40% lift in organic install volume within 60–90 days of ASO changes. SEO content takes 3–6 months to rank but compounds indefinitely.

**Resources needed:** 1 marketer (1 week for ASO audit + copy), content writer (1 landing page), no dev work required.

### Idea 4 — Affiliate / Marketplace de Crédito Channel Optimization (#54)
**Adapted for Koin:** Treat existing Marketplaces de Crédito as a performance partnership — audit activation rates by sub-source, then renegotiate top performers to a CPA-on-activation model rather than CPL (cost per lead). Pause sources with low activation rates.

**Why it fits:** If Marketplaces de Crédito are currently paid on CPL (install or Cadastro), Koin is paying for users who fail credit eval at 65%. Shifting to CPA-on-activation aligns incentives entirely: the platform only earns when it sends users who actually transact. Additionally, activation rates likely vary substantially across sources — without attribution at the activation level, this variance is invisible and budget flows to volume rather than quality.

**How to start:**
1. Pull data: for each marketplace sub-source, calculate install-to-activation rate. Rank sources by activation efficiency (not install volume)
2. Renegotiate with top 1–2 performers on CPA-on-activation model; use the data to make the case
3. Pause or cap spend on sources with activation rates below the channel median

**Expected outcome:** 20–30% reduction in blended CAC (activation basis) within one quarter, without reducing total activations.

**Resources needed:** Data/Analytics (channel attribution at activation level), Business Development (1–2 partnership renegotiations), 2–4 weeks.

### Idea 5 — UGC / Short-Form Video + Comment Marketing (#36, #44)
**Adapted for Koin:** Systematically capture real user testimonials — "I had no credit card and I bought [product] in installments with Koin" — and deploy them as short-form Reels/TikTok content organically and as paid Meta creatives. Pair with a weekly community engagement cadence (Reclame Aqui, relevant Facebook groups).

**Why it fits:** Classes C/D/E audiences are deeply skeptical of financial product advertising — they have been burned by hidden fees and false promises. The most credible signal for this audience is a person who looks like them, speaking to camera, describing how they paid for something they needed. UGC creatives routinely outperform polished ads by 20–40% on CPR for mass-market financial products in Brazil. Reclame Aqui is a critical reputation surface that new users check before downloading any fintech app.

**How to start:**
1. Identify 5–10 highly satisfied activated users (from NPS data or high-value repeat purchasers). Reach out via WhatsApp: "Record a 30-second video about your first purchase." Offer a limit increase as incentive
2. Test UGC creatives against current polished Meta ads in a 2-week A/B test with equal budget split
3. Set up a weekly 30-minute slot to respond on Reclame Aqui and in relevant Facebook groups. Tone: helpful, human, not corporate

**Expected outcome:** 20–40% reduction in CPR on Meta for UGC creatives vs. polished ads. Improved App Store conversion from social proof accumulation over time.

**Resources needed:** 1 community/social manager (part-time), R$500–R$1,000/month in user incentives, Meta Ads access.

## Part 2: Channel Prioritization — Where to Double Down

### Channel 1 — Organic (ASO + Word-of-Mouth Referral)
This is the compounding channel. ASO improvements pay dividends permanently. Every activated user who refers a friend reduces CAC toward zero over time. The context document describes B2B-origin users as "higher quality" — this is the demographic signal that organic/referred users convert better. The strategic bet is that building organic infrastructure (ASO, referral mechanics, UGC content) creates a flywheel that reduces dependence on paid acquisition over 12–18 months. Team time allocation: 30% and growing, even when absolute volume is still low.

### Channel 2 — Lifecycle / CRM (Email + Push + WhatsApp)
This is not an acquisition channel — it is the highest-ROI lever on Koin's primary KPI. A 10-point improvement in activation rate (from 30% to 40% of with-limit users completing a first purchase) is equivalent to a 33% increase in acquisition efficiency across all channels simultaneously. WhatsApp open rates in Brazil exceed 90%. A well-timed message on Day 3 after limit approval will drive more first purchases than any paid ad. This channel deserves the highest copy and strategy investment in the next 90 days.

### Channel 3 — Meta Paid Ads (with optimization, not scale)
Meta remains the volume engine for mobile app acquisition in classes C/D/E Brazil. It should not be abandoned, but it needs two fixes before scaling: (a) shift creatives to UGC to reduce CPR, and (b) optimize campaign objectives for activation events rather than installs — this requires passing the activation event back to Meta via MMP (AppsFlyer/Adjust) so the algorithm learns what a good user looks like. Without these changes, more Meta spend produces more low-quality installs. With them, the same budget should produce more activations within 30–60 days.

**Channels to deprioritize or hold flat:** Google UAC (lower intent volume for this audience vs. Meta), Marketplaces de Crédito with low activation rates (audit first), any brand campaign investment until the activation funnel is more efficient.

## Part 3: 90-Day Marketing Roadmap

### Days 1–30: Quick Wins — Fix the Funnel Before Scaling It

**Initiative 1 — Activation Sequence (CRM)**
Deploy the 5-message activation sprint for with-limit users. This is the fastest path to improving the primary KPI with zero additional acquisition spend.
- Owner: CRM/Lifecycle
- Metric: Activation rate for with-limit cohort (week-over-week)
- Success threshold: +5 percentage points within 30 days of deploy

**Initiative 2 — Meta UGC Creative Test**
Recruit 5 UGC creators from the user base. Run a 2-week A/B test vs. current polished creatives with equal budget split.
- Owner: Paid Media + Social
- Metric: CPR (activation basis), CTR
- Success threshold: UGC CPR equal or lower than control within 14 days

**Initiative 3 — Channel Attribution Audit**
Pull activation rates by sub-source for all Marketplaces de Crédito. Produce a ranked table: source / installs / credit-eval pass rate / activation rate / CAC on activation basis.
- Owner: Data/Analytics
- This is a diagnostic. Output is the ranked table. Deadline: end of Week 2.

---

### Days 31–60: Mid-Term Bets — Build the Flywheel

**Initiative 1 — Referral Program Launch**
Ship "Indica e Ganha" mechanic. Reward confirmed, referral link generated post first purchase, WhatsApp sharing copy live.
- Owner: Product + Marketing
- Metric: Referral attempt rate per activated user, referred-user activation rate vs. channel baseline
- Success threshold: 10%+ of activated users attempt a referral within 30 days of launch

**Initiative 2 — ASO Refresh**
Rewrite App Store and Google Play listings. Update screenshots to feature Shopee, iFood, MercadoLibre, Amazon explicitly.
- Owner: Marketing (ASO)
- Metric: Organic install volume, App Store keyword rankings
- Success threshold: 15%+ increase in organic installs within 45 days

**Initiative 3 — Marketplace Channel Rebalancing**
Based on the attribution audit, renegotiate with top performers on CPA-activation model. Pause lowest-performing sources.
- Owner: BizDev + Data
- Metric: Blended CAC — Marketplaces channel (activation basis)
- Success threshold: 15% reduction in blended CAC within this channel

**Initiative 4 — No-Limit Reactivation Flow**
Deploy 2-message reactivation sequence for no-limit cohort. Target users rejected in the last 90 days.
- Owner: CRM/Lifecycle
- Metric: Reactivation rate of no-limit cohort
- Success threshold: 3–8% reactivation within 60 days

---

### Days 61–90: Longer-Term Compounding Play

**Initiative 1 — SEO Landing Page**
Publish one landing page targeting "parcelamento sem cartão de crédito" and "como usar Pix parcelado". Plain Portuguese, concrete examples, no financial jargon. CTA to download.
- Owner: Content + Marketing
- Metric: Organic search impressions, keyword ranking position, organic install attribution
- Note: Will not show ranking results within 90 days. Publish by Day 75 and treat as a 6-month investment.

**Initiative 2 — UGC Community Presence**
Establish a weekly cadence: Reclame Aqui responses, relevant Facebook group participation, comment engagement on fintech content.
- Owner: Social/Community (part-time)
- Metric: Reclame Aqui score, brand sentiment
- Success threshold: Reclame Aqui score above 7.0 within 90 days

## Critical Assumptions to Validate

1. **B2B-origin users activate at a higher rate than paid channels** — must be confirmed with data before doubling down on organic/referral
2. **The limit→activation gap is a friction/awareness problem, not a product problem** — 5 usability tests with limit-holders who haven't purchased will answer this cheaply
3. **UGC creatives outperform polished ads for this audience on Meta** — strong hypothesis, requires the 2-week A/B test to confirm
4. **The no-limit cohort has reactivation potential** — confirm with credit/risk team whether eligibility shifts within 90 days before investing in the reactivation flow

---

## One-Line Strategic Summary

Fix the activation funnel before scaling acquisition: the 65% of users who fail credit eval cannot be marketed to, so the priority is extracting maximum value from the 35% who can transact — through lifecycle sequences, referral mechanics, and creative optimization — while building the organic and ASO infrastructure that will compound CAC reduction over the next 12 months.
